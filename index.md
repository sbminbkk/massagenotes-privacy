# Privacy Policy - Massage Briefing

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

Massage Briefing helps people record massage session preferences and share a report they choose to create. It does not require an account.

## Information you enter

The app saves the profile name you enter, muscle selections, markup, session preferences, and notes in storage on your device. The app uses that information to display your sessions and prepare reports. It does not operate a notes or profile server. Your device's backup or transfer services may also preserve app data according to your device settings. If you choose to save a report to a location on your device, that location is controlled by the device's file picker.

## Reports you choose to share

When you use Share, the app gives the report image to the app you select in your device's share sheet. The receiving app handles that image under its own privacy practices. Massage Briefing does not send a report to another person on its own.

## Updates and technical diagnostics

The app contacts Expo's update service to check for compatible updates. Update requests include technical information such as platform, app runtime and channel, and a randomly generated installation identifier saved in app preferences that remains stable across launches and updates. Expo also receives the device's IP address and stores request records. It uses this information to deliver compatible updates, manage update rollouts, and measure installations and update failures. Expo states that it does not derive a location from these requests. In a rare update recovery path, a previous fatal error message may also be sent to Expo. See [Expo's update-data explanation](https://docs.expo.dev/eas-update/store-privacy-declarations/).

Release versions send crash and technical error information to Sentry to help diagnose and fix problems. Technical reports can include stack traces, app-version information, and device or operating-system characteristics. The app filters JavaScript error events before sending: it replaces free-form error text, removes console and network breadcrumbs and request details, and retains selected technical fields. Native crash reports and Expo update-recovery messages use separate technical reporting paths. These safeguards reduce information in diagnostics; this policy does not promise that all technical error context is anonymous or free of entered information.

Sentry also receives the network connection's IP address and can infer approximate geographic context from it. We have configured project-side scrubbing of IP addresses and city, region, subdivision, and country fields in stored events. This filtering does not prevent the service from receiving the connection or promise that no geographic processing occurs before filtering. Native diagnostic reports can include a randomly generated installation identifier.

The app does not use advertising or an advertising identifier. It does not request your device's location, contacts, camera, or microphone. It does not provide a sign-in account or collect payment information. The technical diagnostics above are used to monitor app health and fix problems; they are not advertising analytics.

## Retention and deleting information

Local profile and session information remains on your device until you delete it or clear the app's storage. You can clear the app's storage or uninstall the app through your device settings. A report you saved or shared may remain in the destination you selected. Copies managed by your device's backup or transfer services follow those services' settings.

Clearing app storage does not itself delete diagnostics previously sent to Sentry or Expo. The app currently provides no tool for requesting deletion of individual diagnostic or update records. You can contact the privacy address below with questions about this information.

Our current Sentry plan is Developer. Sentry's published retention period for new error events on this plan is 30 days. Retention is assigned when an event is received, so events recorded during an earlier trial or on another plan can keep their original retention period. This describes event retention and does not promise that every backup or other provider record is deleted at the same time. See [Sentry's retention explanation](https://www.sentry.help/en/articles/13964323-how-long-are-errors-events-stored-in-sentry).

Expo states that raw request logs containing update-recovery error messages are deleted after three days. That period applies to those logs, not automatically to every installation identifier or update-request record; the remaining records follow Expo's retention practices described in its [privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy).

## Security

The app's configured technical-service endpoints use HTTPS to encrypt data in transit. Local information and device-managed backups are subject to your device's storage and security settings.

## Children

Massage Briefing is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
