# Privacy Policy — MassageNotes

_Last updated: July 10, 2026_

MassageNotes is a personal note-taking app for massage therapists and their clients. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

## The short version

**Everything you enter stays on your device.** MassageNotes has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server that receives your data.

## Information the app stores

- **Profile name** — the name you enter on the profile screen.
- **Session notes** — muscle selections, markup, notes, and session preferences you record.

All of this is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

## Information the app does not collect

- No analytics or tracking.
- No advertising identifiers.
- No location, contacts, camera, or microphone data.
- No account or login information (there are no accounts).

## App updates

The app periodically contacts Expo's update servers (expo.dev) to check whether a newer version of the app is available. This is a standard update check; no personal data or note content is sent.

## Sharing reports

When you use the app's share feature, the report image is handed to the app **you choose** (e.g., a messaging app). What happens after that is governed by that app's privacy policy. MassageNotes never sends reports anywhere on its own.

## Deleting your data

Your data lives only on your device. To delete it, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. There is no server-side copy to delete.

## Children

MassageNotes is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example, adding crash reporting), this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised.
